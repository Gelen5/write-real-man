# Industry observation drafting brief

This is an internal brief, not an article skeleton. Do not print its field names in the article.

- Concrete event, event date versus publication date, and direct source.
- One traceable object and unresolved question with a human consequence.
- Three evidenced applications: what each receives, changes and hands onward.
- For each connection: observed, inferred, proposed, or unknown.
- Why each additional source is needed at that point in the argument.
- A reasoned author choice; a proposed next step and its limitation.
- End at a concrete action or unresolved decision. Do not append a significance summary.

Headings follow the material. No required five sections, data wall, serial number or invented byline. Preserve sources beside claims. Keep editorial notes separate from the locked publication text.
