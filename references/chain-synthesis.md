# Chain synthesis

An industry chain is a sequence of handoffs, not a collection of AI examples.

## Layer card

`input → operation → output → recipient → human decision → failure mode`

Name the transferred object: sensor data, image, risk score, work order, alert, verified identity, payment instruction, or physical dispatch. “Data collaboration” is too vague.

## Opportunity test

An opportunity is stronger when four things are visible:

1. a repeated break or expensive manual handoff;
2. a buyer or responsible institution;
3. a measurable result or avoided loss;
4. a constraint that prevents instant replication.

Do not infer a market merely because a technology exists. Show the procurement, staffing pressure, compliance work, maintenance, integration, or recurring verification that reveals a buyer.

## Counterfactual test

Ask what changes without each layer. If removing it changes nothing, it may be decorative. If AI cannot change a decision, time, uncertainty, coverage, or coordination, do not make it the hero.
