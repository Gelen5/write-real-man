# Industry topic selection

Score candidate events only after deduplicating reports of the same origin. Scores guide editorial choice; they are not platform statistics or click-through predictions.

| Dimension | Weight | Question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Event specificity | 20 | Is there a dated action, conflict, failure, or measurable result? |
| Evidence strength | 20 | Is the central fact supported by an authoritative original? |
| Chainability | 20 | Can at least three distinct layers and two real handoffs be researched? |
| Stakes | 15 | What changes for a person, institution, public service, cost, safety, or responsibility? |
| Cognitive gap | 15 | Does the evidence support a conclusion that differs from the obvious headline? |
| Freshness | 10 | Is the event or new evidence inside the declared window? |

Reject topics built on a broad label, one unsupported viral post, a product announcement without use or consequence, or three cases joined only by the word AI.

## Title gate

A candidate title should expose the event before explaining the technology. Prefer `number/time + action/consequence` or `unexpected actor + concrete problem`. Do not insert drama that the evidence cannot support.

For each shortlisted topic state the event anchor, why it matters now, proposed chain, non-obvious thesis, missing evidence, main risk, and direct sources.
