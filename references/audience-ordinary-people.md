# Audience: ordinary people

Start with the user's stated reader. If none is stated, default to ordinary office workers and everyday AI users. Do not silently assume programmers.

Common reader lenses include office workers, parents, students, creators, solo business owners, sales staff, administrators, teachers, small-business owners, freelancers and nontechnical founders.

Ask what they already have and what they need next. The same feature can solve different problems: an image tool may help a creator prepare a cover, a parent keep a growth record, a small shop improve a product photo, or a teacher make a simple classroom illustration.

Avoid stereotypes. A job title suggests possible constraints; it does not prove an individual has a specific problem or tool access.
