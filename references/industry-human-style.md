# Human style for evidence-led industry writing

Human feel comes from judgment under evidence, not casual filler.

## Use the event as the spine

Return to the same clock, place, person, machine, document, or decision. A date matters when it marks a change: an anomaly was found, an alert arrived, people moved, a system failed, or authority changed hands.

## Let paragraphs do different jobs

One paragraph may reconstruct an action; the next examines a number; the next challenges a tempting conclusion. Avoid equal sections that each contain a definition, three benefits, and a summary.

## Show the seam

Credible passages expose where one system hands work to another, where data stops, or where a person must decide. Name that seam instead of saying “形成闭环” or “实现协同”.

## Write judgment with a reason

Good: “我更在意那 23 天。设备发现异常并不等于完成避险，真正改变结果的是监测、上报和转移在同一条时间线上接上了。”

Weak: “这一案例充分体现了科技赋能的巨大价值。”

The first interprets evidence. The second only praises it.

## Preserve rough reality

Keep partial deployment, waiting, false alarms, manual work, missing interfaces, regional limits, and unresolved responsibility when sources show them. Do not turn every pilot into a mature industry.

## Avoid detector theatre

Do not add typos, random slang, forced interjections, fragments, or scheduled phrases such as “说实话”. Keep syntax complete and citations intact. Revise weak paragraph functions before tone.
