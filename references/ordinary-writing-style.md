# Ordinary-language writing

Write so a reader can follow the task without first learning AI vocabulary.

- Use everyday nouns and verbs. Name the file, message, receipt, form or list.
- Let a concrete scene start the article; do not manufacture a personal anecdote.
- Put instructions beside the moment they are needed.
- Use one natural, copyable prompt rather than a default `Role / Context / Task / Constraints / Output` form.
- Show what the AI returned and the exact correction when the reader needs to learn checking.
- Keep limitations attached to the relevant action. Do not end every section with a lesson.
- Use technical terms only when a reader must recognize them in the interface. Explain once in plain language.

Avoid generic AI marketing terms unless a sourced claim requires them: 赋能、生产力革命、范式、生态、全链路、闭环能力、智能体革命、技术演进. Replace them with the action, time, money or decision that actually changes.

Use complete sentences and punctuation. Do not imitate typos, random slang or detector-scoring tricks. A useful article can be warm without pretending to be the author's diary.
