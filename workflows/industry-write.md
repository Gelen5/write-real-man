# Workflow: Write the observation article

Read `../references/validated-sanitation-case.md` before drafting. Choose one traceable object for an operational chain. Section headings and summary cards are optional; preserve continuous prose when it carries the argument. The article skeleton is a research checklist, not a requirement to display every section.

## Opening

Begin with the event's consequential moment. Give a time, place, action, or number. Ask one real question: why did the chain work, fail, or become possible? Do not begin with “随着 AI 发展”, a definition, or a technology list.

## Body movement

Each section must change understanding: reconstruct the timeline, widen to the pressure, open a layer, show its handoff, mark machine and human authority, derive an opportunity from a missing link, then test it against a constraint.

Prefer two or three strong bottlenecks over an exhaustive risk list. Put source links beside the supported claim. Keep exact dates and numbers stable.

## Author voice

Use first-person judgment only to choose the decisive detail, correct an overstatement, or separate what exists from what could exist. Natural self-correction is useful when it sharpens a boundary. Do not manufacture uncertainty for style.

## Ending

Return to the person, institution, or physical action at the end of the chain. Stop when the consequence is clear. Do not repeat the article or add a generic call to embrace AI.
