# Workflow: Article Research

Research only after the topic has been selected; trend discovery is intentionally lightweight.

1. Split claims into product facts, community reports, user-provided experience and interpretation.
2. Verify product behavior, release dates, prices, account/region/plan availability and safety claims against current official sources.
3. Read the original community item when using a user story. Preserve its date and context; label it as one report, not general demand.
4. Keep uncertain details explicit. Never fabricate a fictional user's experience, live availability, result or quote.
5. Record exact URLs, dates and protected technical terms in a research pack based on `references/research-pack-schema.md`.
6. If the example is a constructed exercise, say so near the example.

Follow `references/source-quality.md` and `references/fact-integrity.md`.
