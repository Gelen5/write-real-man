# Workflow: Build the industry chain

Start with evidence cards, not an outline.

For every layer record input, operation, output, next recipient, decision owner, failure mode, and evidence state. Connect adjacent layers only when one output can realistically become the next input. Shared vocabulary is not a handoff.

Maintain three maps:

1. **Observed chain** — demonstrated by sources.
2. **Inferred chain** — logically supported but not documented as deployed.
3. **Proposed chain** — the author's design for a fuller system or opportunity.

Use explicit language: “通报显示” for observed facts, “由此可以推断” for inference, “我的设想是” for proposals, and “公开材料没有说明” for unknowns.

Proceed only with one authoritative event anchor, at least three layers, at least two plausible handoffs, one clear consequence, and one meaningful bottleneck. Otherwise narrow the thesis or research again.
