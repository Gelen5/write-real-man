# Workflow: Industry research

## Search in five lanes

1. **Event anchor** — government notice, regulator release, official incident report, original company release, or original paper.
2. **Adjacent applications** — verified deployments that perform neighboring steps in the same chain.
3. **Scale and context** — official statistics, public procurement, disclosed-method reports, or authoritative media.
4. **Operational boundary** — laws, standards, responsibility, false positives, integration, staffing, cost, and maintenance.
5. **Practitioner signal** — attributed posts and interviews used only to show a concern, workflow, or reaction.

Chinese sources are first-class. Search ministries, local governments, official media, industry bodies, universities, companies, public WeChat articles, Zhihu, Weibo, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, Douyin, Toutiao, Juejin, and 36Kr when accessible. Use global primary sources when they add a missing layer.

## Research order

1. Fix `as_of_date`, time, and place.
2. Find the earliest authoritative account.
3. Extract only claims that source supports.
4. Search every missing link in the proposed chain separately.
5. Open originals behind media summaries whenever possible.
6. Record disagreement, stale numbers, coverage limits, and unavailable sources.
7. Stop when every major paragraph has evidence or an explicit inference/proposal label.

Do not draft from search snippets. An announcement does not prove adoption, demand, or effectiveness.
